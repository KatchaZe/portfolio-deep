fix(engine): terminal-phase realism + data-integrity guards (build 2026-08-03a)

Chased one anomaly (PFE reading +151% undervalued) and found the Fundamental
PEG path was systematically rating LOW-quality companies as the cheapest. Root
cause: a hardcoded 15% perpetuity ROIC handed to every company. Before the fix
the biggest "upsides" in the portfolio were REGN +110%, ABBV +34%, ELV +29% —
the three lowest-ROIC names that had a fair value at all.

Fixing that exposed a chain of related defects, all the same shape: a constant
applied universally, a guard that vanishes when its input is missing, or a clamp
that only ever pushes one way. Each is tagged in the code (P-A, P-D, S1, R1,
REVIEW-1, T2, ...) with the reasoning and the numbers that motivated it.

Terminal phase now fades RETURNS, GROWTH and RISK together
- D  terminal_roic() = max(WACC, min(ROIC, industry cap, WACC + half the spread));
     the cost of capital is the floor, so a below-WACC business is not written
     off forever and a moatless one is never gifted excess returns
- J  new sources/damodaran.py fetches his "Return on Capital by Sector (US)"
     table each refresh (30-day disk cache, bundled Jan-2026 snapshot fallback,
     columns located by HEADER TEXT, never raises). Uses the normalized 10-year
     column: the single-year one is an aggregate over every filer in the bucket
     and "Drugs (Biotechnology)" is 496 firms, mostly pre-revenue burners
- K  terminal_beta() = clamp(beta, 0.8, 1.2), applied in BOTH directions
     (NVDA 2.21 -> 1.2, REGN 0.24 -> 0.8). Leaving beta un-faded was what gave a
     62%-ROIC company a stable-phase P/E of 8.3
- B2 two_stage_pe(ke_st=) / stable_exit_pe capitalize the perpetuity at the
     stable-phase Ke while the high-growth years keep the current one
- L  reverse_dcf(wacc_term=, roic_term=) — it still had the hardcoded 15% in the
     reinvestment term and capitalized the perpetuity at the high-growth WACC.
     Terminal value is 90-97% of PV there, so this dominated: HIMS implied CAGR
     22.8% -> 11.0%. Both args default to the previous behaviour
- H  g <= ROIC. max(0, 1 - g/ROIC) silently floored the shortfall, so MELI at
     g=30%/ROIC=13.7% needed 219% of earnings reinvested, paid no dilution, and
     still scaled terminal earnings by (1+g)^5
- E  growth input = max(0, min(3y, 5y CAGR)). The old full-span CAGR measured
     straight through structural breaks: PFE +8.5% from a 2020 pre-COVID base
     while its 3y CAGR was -14.8%
- M  PE clamps unified to [5, 35] for both paths (were [8,30] and [12,25]). The
     old floor bound 11 of 21 holdings in the FVP path and gifted up to +103%

Data integrity — no guess is silent, no guard fails open
- A  EBIT = pretax + interest when a filer tags no operating-income subtotal
     (LLY, PFE), for the TTM scalar, annuals and quarters. Without it both read
     as pre-profit despite $25.3B and $7.5B of net income
- T  market_cap is now captured from FMP/Yahoo and shares are DERIVED as
     market_cap / price. Both are quoted for the US-listed security, so the
     depositary ratio is already inside them — no ADR-ratio table to maintain.
     When a SEC count also exists the two are cross-checked; a mismatch reports
     the implied ratio (TSM: 5.00x) instead of silently corrupting market cap,
     the WACC weights and enterprise value at once
- S1/R1 ONE forward-EPS gate shared by validate and normalize, with a
     shares-free implied-P/E band. The revenue-capacity ceiling needs a share
     count and simply disappeared without one — TSM's 323.34 (TWD per ordinary
     share against a $398 USD ADR price, a 1.2x forward P/E) sailed through and
     was driving fv_peg = $5,856
- R3 that ceiling now grows with expected growth; comparing a FORWARD EPS to
     CURRENT revenue per share was systematically tight and rejected NVDA's
     real consensus
- C  "Pre-profit" is only said when net income is actually negative
- F  fundamental growth (reinvestment x ROIC) as a divergence FLAG, honest about
     the fact that the capex-based formula reads 0% for asset-light R&D spenders
- G  a broken FVP now discards only that method instead of nulling the anchor
     and taking a sound PEG down with it (ABBV: a $23 FVP killed a $210 PEG)
- S3 the degraded "WACC=Ke, no weights" path and a missing share count are
     flagged instead of passing silently

Post-review fixes (mattpocock-skills:code-review over the whole diff)
- REVIEW-1 `growth_lt or 0.08` read a MEASURED zero as missing, so every
     decliner silently got the 8% default and defeated E
- REVIEW-2 terminal_roic() returned the raw industry number when the firm's own
     ROIC was unknown — Tobacco 77.7% as a gift
- REVIEW-3 the terminal ROIC floor used today's WACC while the perpetuity is
     capitalized at the terminal one
- REVIEW-4 the ke_st - g >= 3% bound does NOT cap the formula near 35; raw P/Es
     of 40-56 are reachable. Corrected the comment; the cap stays and is flagged
- REVIEW-5 analyze_row never passed roc_table, so adding one ticker used the
     bundled snapshot while a batch refresh used the live table
- REVIEW-6 fundamental_peg_price rejected g <= 0, so once REVIEW-1 let a real 0%
     through, no-growth firms lost their PEG valuation entirely (PFE swung 94pp)
- R4 _add_flag() replaces ~14 copies of the dedup idiom — and fixes an EQ-flag
     loop that tested one string and appended another, so it never deduped

Effect: the anti-quality bias is gone. REGN +109 -> +29%, ELV +28 -> -33%,
