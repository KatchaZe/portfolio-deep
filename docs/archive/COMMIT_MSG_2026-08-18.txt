fix(validate): a 200x forward P/E is an expensive stock, not a broken unit (A16)

Found on the LIVE Render deploy (build 2026-08-17a) on 2026-08-18 by reading
TSLA's flags off /api/portfolio in the browser. Invisible on a local run.

SYMPTOM
-------
TSLA and AXON showed no fair value, no PEG and no Future-Value Projection.
Their flags said:

  TSLA  forward_eps 1.69 rejected (implied forward P/E 200.6x outside [3, 200]
        - currency or share-unit mismatch vs price 339.3);
        SEC fallback 1.13 rejected too (300.0x) - PEG valuation skipped
  AXON  no consensus, and SEC-derived 3.02 rejected (implied forward P/E 200.2x
        outside [3, 200] ... vs price 604.32) - PEG valuation skipped

              price      fwd EPS   implied P/E   over the ceiling by
  TSLA      $339.30    1.69 FMP        200.6x                 +0.3%
  AXON      $604.32    3.02 SEC        200.2x                 +0.1%

Two of 21 live rows lost their entire per-share valuation path and fell back to
reverse DCF alone.

ROOT CAUSE
----------
pipeline/validate.py used ONE symmetric band, FWD_PE_MIN, FWD_PE_MAX = 3.0, 200.0,
and labelled every rejection "currency or share-unit mismatch". The two sides do
not fail the same way:

  * LOW side really is the currency test. An unconverted local currency always
    makes EPS TOO LARGE (TWD, JPY, KRW, DKK all take many units to the dollar),
    so the tell is a P/E far below anything a real business trades at (TSM 1.2x).
    This bound is correct and unchanged.

  * HIGH side cannot be a currency artefact at all - no FX rate makes EPS smaller.
    What it catches is a NEAR-ZERO forward EPS, where PEG and FVP divide by noise
    (RKLB: 0.01 against $80 = 9,608x). But 200x sits INSIDE the range real, very
    expensive equities trade at. A unit artefact is off by a FACTOR; TSLA and AXON
    were off by a fraction of a percent.

FIX
---
The high side splits in two:

    FWD_PE_MIN, FWD_PE_MAX = 3.0, 600.0   # reject: currency (low) / near-zero EPS (high)
    FWD_PE_EXTREME = 200.0                # keep the value above this, but never silently

New forward_eps_extreme() is the counterpart to forward_eps_rejection(): between
200x and 600x the value REACHES the valuation and the row carries an explicit flag
saying the multiple is extreme and PEG/FVP are assumption-sensitive, so the reader
weighs them against the reverse DCF.

All THREE accept branches in _resolve_forward_eps route through _flag_extreme() -
flagging only the first would be the same bug class as L1 (2026-08-10).

per_share_unit_mismatch() is untouched: it only ever tested the low side, and its
docstring now says the two functions share the LOW bound only.

WHY 14 ROUNDS OF REVIEW MISSED IT
---------------------------------
Locally the blend has two sources - Yahoo 2.175 + FMP 1.6913 -> 1.9331, an implied
175.5x, comfortably inside the old band. On Render, Yahoo blocks datacenter IPs
(source census: local yahoo 21/21, Render yahoo 1/21), so TSLA had FMP's 1.69 alone
and tipped over the edge.

A defect that only appears with a DEGRADED SOURCE SET cannot be found by running
the suite against a full local store. REVIEW_PROCESS.md gains checklist items
16 (run at least one source-starved store), 17 (every threshold must answer whether
the fault it catches distorts by a FACTOR or by a PERCENT - and if real values can
reach it, warn rather than discard) and 18 (read flags on production, not just the
displayed numbers - A16 was only visible in a flag; the page showed a blank).
