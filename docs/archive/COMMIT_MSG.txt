THE THREE THAT CHANGED THE MOST NUMBERS
- REV-1  f.sbc was never populated on the live path. SEC did not fetch
         ShareBasedCompensation and fmp.parse() (the only setter) is called zero
         times in pipeline/ or domain/. So the "SBC >10% of revenue" branch in
         earnings_quality() was dead code, and the framework's own rule that SBC
         must be charged as dilution had never been implemented at all.
- REV-2  When ROIC could not be measured the code handed out ROIC_TERMINAL 15% AND
         the full GROWTH_CAP 30%. The docstring claimed such names fall through to
         the reverse DCF; they do not. A company with negative invested capital
         (cash > equity + debt, routine for cash-rich software and biotech) has
         positive NOPAT and lands squarely on the PEG path. The firms we cannot
         measure were getting the most generous defaults available.
         Net-cash case: FV $39.72 -> $20.79 (-48%), HOLD/Accumulate -> HOLD.
- REV-8  composite() renormalises over the AVAILABLE pillars, so a missing fair
         value does not penalise a name - it deletes the price test. D4/E4/E4 with
         P1 scores 3.10 (HOLD); the same three pillars with P missing score 4.00
         (BUY). A Yahoo outage on forward EPS was UPGRADING the recommendation, and
         the more expensive the stock the bigger the upgrade.
         cap_reco_without_price() now requires a price opinion for a BUY.

TERMINAL PHASE AND THE REVERSE DCF
- REV-3/28  reinvestment in the reverse DCF was capped at 0.9, so every CAGR from
            0.9xROIC to 100% was charged identical reinvestment and FCFF stopped
            responding to growth. Raising the cap to 1.0 did not fix it - at x=ROIC
            interim FCFF hits zero and stays there, leaving the model 100% terminal
            value above that point. The cap did not remove the flatness, it moved
            it, and it silently defeated the REV-24 margin ramp. Removed entirely:
            growth beyond what ROIC funds is bought with outside capital, so FCFF
            must be allowed to go negative.
            Removing it exposed an older bug: implied() bisected [-50%, +100%]
            assuming pv_at was monotonic. It is UNIMODAL - value rises while the
            return on growth beats its cost, peaks, then falls as the reinvestment
            bill outruns profit. Every thin-spread firm (ROIC ~ WACC, i.e. exactly
            S5's "growth is worthless without a spread") came back out-of-band at
            any price. Now: locate the peak, bisect the rising branch, and
            distinguish "no growth rate justifies this price" from "needs >100%/yr".
- REV-12    EV excluded capitalised operating leases that the WACC weights and
            invested capital already included - the reverse DCF was valuing a
            different firm from the one the discount rate was priced for.
- REV-24    the margin was held at the TERMINAL value from year 1. Defensible for a
            company already near its terminal margin; for a pre-profit filer it
            credited a loss-making business with an assumed 25% margin next year.
            Now ramps from today's, matching young_dcf.
- REV-25    reverse_dcf recomputed price x shares while the engine had already
            resolved the reported market cap (T4). _resolve_shares only intervenes
            past a 20% gap, so up to 20% of disagreement passed silently.
- REV-17    terminal_roic()'s rule 3 ("the cost of capital is the FLOOR") was
            skipped on the roic_now-is-None branch. Survivable while that branch
            only fed the perpetuity; REV-2 had just made it load-bearing.
- REV-26    a hit on the coarse GICS sector map is not an industry view. Mapping
            all of "healthcare" onto Drugs (Pharmaceutical) 23.52% handed every
            unmapped healthcare name the ceiling of one of its richest corners.
            Coarse hits are now capped at the market-wide normalized ROIC.

SCORING AND MEASUREMENT
- REV-4   spread_prior used lease-FREE invested capital while spread used
          lease-capitalised IC, so a lease-heavy filer was measured against a
          smaller past capital base and showed a deterioration that never happened.
          Worth up to -1.0 on E_econ, the 30%-weighted pillar.
- REV-7   the Price pillar divided by PRICE and called it margin of safety. MoS is
          (Value - Price)/VALUE (appendix A, S4/S12). Dividing by price distorts
          the bands asymmetrically: the +40% cut was really a 28.6% margin (too easy
          to reach) and the -40% cut a -66.7% one (nearly unreachable).
- REV-11  ke_eff = max(ke, rf + 3.5%) was a floor with no matching ceiling. It
          raised the discount rate for every beta below ~0.79 and never restrained
          a beta of 2.2 - and it contradicted its own docstring, since P-K already
          fades risk properly in both directions via terminal_beta.
- REV-9/18 reinvestment was capex - D&A only. Damodaran's is capex + acquisitions +
          dWC - D&A. acquisitions_net was already fetched and unused; dWC is now
          derived from the BALANCE SHEET (AR + inventory - AP), not from
          IncreaseDecreaseInOperatingCapital, whose sign convention varies by filer.
- REV-19  receivables carried the comment "AR vs revenue (channel-stuffing check)"
          since v8.2 and was read by nothing. The check needed the prior-year AR,
          which is why it was never written.
- REV-23  the 0.8 reinvestment ceiling guaranteed fcff >= 20% of NOPAT, so the
          cash-burn gate could never fire for a profitable company - the one thing
          it existed to detect.
- REV-27  pick() tie-broke on largest |TTM|, a magnitude preference dressed up as a
          rule. A filer tagging both Revenues and the ASC 606 concept got the
          broader number. Now list order, with a conflict flag - which immediately
          caught a real ORCL net-income conflict in the committed fixtures.
- REV-5/6/10/13/14/20  out-of-band silently reported as "Unknown"; sensitivity
          solved twice per scenario; falsy-zero guard on net_income; unbounded
          incremental ROIC; a note labelled 5y that measured 1y; the terminal-margin
          band [5%, 40%] binding without saying so.

NEW: PRE-PROFIT FORWARD VALUATION (S20)  -  REV-16
Pre-profit names had anchor_value = None and no Price pillar. The only thing on the
card was the reverse DCF, which its own docstring calls "a PRICING cross-check, NOT
a valuation". domain/engine/young_dcf.py implements Damodaran's young-company
method: failure risk as a separate probability (never in the discount rate), growth
paid for through the sales-to-capital ratio, and the answer as a distribution.

Every input is derived from filed data - survival from cash runway, distress from
net cash, dilution from the share count, sales-to-capital from revenue/IC. The
Monte Carlo is seeded, so an unchanged refresh cannot move the fair value.

Per the agreed rule, the p50 becomes the anchor ONLY when the band is tight enough
to mean something (p50 > 0, p10 > 0, p90/p10 < 4x); otherwise it stays
informational and the row falls back to the previous behaviour, with the reason
stated. index.html draws the band, the survival decomposition and the priced-in
comparison (youngPanel / pricedInLine, build 2026-08-04a).

SECOND-PASS AUDIT OF THIS SESSION'S OWN CODE  -  P2-1..P2-4
A fresh read found three MED/HIGH defects in the code written above:
- P2-1  the SBC/market-cap dilution proxy measures GROSS grants and moves with the
        share PRICE - the same grant is charged double the dilution after the stock
        halves. The real WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding series was
        in the same companyfacts JSON all along (10-18 annual points per US filer)
        and disagrees in SIGN for MSFT (proxy +0.39%/yr vs actual -0.05%) and ABBV
        (+0.28% vs 0.00%) - both grant heavily and retire more than they issue.
        Real history is now primary (3y CAGR, floored at 0: net buybacks are a
        discretionary policy and must not be capitalised into perpetuity); the
        proxy survives only for filers with no share count at all (IFRS).
- P2-2  dilution was charged to BOTH fair-value paths. fv_peg runs on forward_eps
        (NTM adjusted, non-GAAP, SBC added back) where it belongs; fv_fvp runs on
        eps0 from GAAP net income, where SBC is already an expense - double-counting
        ~12% of value and breaking the framework's own "no double counting" rule.
        Also skipped when validate() substitutes a SEC-derived (GAAP) forward EPS.
- P2-3  working_capital_change returned partial-leg deltas. The legs have opposite
        signs, so a filer tagging only AccountsPayable produced a NEGATIVE dWC -
        "working capital released cash" - purely because the offsetting legs were
        not filed, understating reinvestment in the company's favour. Receivables
        is now required.
- P2-4  the Monte Carlo margin span was a flat +/-6pp, which meant the promotion
        gate was judging our sampling width rather than the company. Now +/-4pp when
        the terminal margin is measured from the firm and +/-10pp when it is the
        generic assumption, and the span is reported alongside the band.
- P2-5  caught by running the suite on the real fixtures: REV-23 removed the
        reinvestment clamp that had made the `fcf_pos` leg of the ROUTING gate
        vacuous, and thereby changed where a profitable heavy-investor gets valued.
        ORCL - $48.2B capex against $16.4B NOPAT, a 268% reinvestment rate and
        -$27.4B FCFF - was being sent to the S20 young-company model and came back
        at $25 against a $213 price. "No earnings to capitalise" and "invested
        heavily this year" are different situations; only the first is what S20
        models. Routing now keys on earnings, and negative FCFF is a loud flag
        instead of a different valuation method. ORCL: $22.68 -> $95.68 via
        Fundamental PEG.

NOTE ON PROCESS
Four of the 32 (REV-17, REV-28, P2-1, P2-2) were regressions introduced by earlier
fixes in this same session. Two patterns to watch: making a value load-bearing
somewhere it was not before, and removing a clamp that was masking a structural
assumption. And before adding a proxy, check whether the real data is already being
downloaded - for the share count, it was.
